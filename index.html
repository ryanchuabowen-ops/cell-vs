How to Play

  • Move: WASD or Arrow Keys
  • Aim: Your cell always faces your mouse cursor
  • Right-click: Primary power — strong, long recharge
  • Left-click: Secondary power — weak, fast, usually unlimited ammo
  • E: Wall Kit — drop a small temporary wall that blocks movement and sight (up to 2 at a time, long recharge)
  • Walls (and drifting debris) block line of sight — fog of war hides anything you can't directly see.
  • PvBot: 10 immune cells vs 10 pathogens on a random biological map (blood vessel, gut, lungs, lymph node). Capture the North/Center/South flags to score over time, or rack up kills — first to 60 points wins, or whoever leads when the clock hits zero.
  • Bots fighting each other far away from you won't settle the match by themselves — getting involved is what makes things happen.
  • PvE Horde: you play a Phage (bacteriophage/virophage) alongside 20 AI immune allies, clearing 8 escalating waves of pathogens.
